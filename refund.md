# BOMYRA — Refund Policy

**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

## 1. General Policy

Payments for BOMYRA subscriptions are generally non-refundable once the payment has been successfully completed.

By purchasing a BOMYRA Pro subscription, you acknowledge this policy, subject to any refund rights or other protections that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

Transactions processed through Paddle are also subject to Paddle's Buyer Terms and Refund Policy. Paddle may, at its discretion, approve a refund in accordance with its own policy.

## 2. Subscription Plans

BOMYRA Pro is available with two billing options:

- **Monthly:** $5.99 USD per month.
- **Yearly:** $39.99 USD per year.

Both options provide the same features. They differ only in billing period and price.

## 3. Automatic Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled before the next renewal.

You may cancel at any time through the subscription management facility provided by Paddle.

Cancelling a subscription stops future automatic renewals but does not immediately end your paid access.

## 4. After Cancellation

When you cancel:

- You retain access to BOMYRA Pro until the end of the current paid billing period.
- Your account does not immediately revert to the Free plan.
- Cancellation does not automatically result in a refund.
- After the paid period expires, your account returns to the Free plan unless you purchase another subscription.

## 5. Changing Your Billing Period

Monthly and Yearly are billing options for the same BOMYRA Pro subscription.

You cannot switch between billing periods while your current subscription is active.

You may select a different billing period after the current subscription has expired.

## 6. Exceptions Required by Law

Although payments are generally non-refundable after completion, this policy does not override mandatory refund rights, withdrawal rights, or other consumer protections that apply under applicable law.

Where applicable law requires a refund or other remedy, those requirements will be respected.
